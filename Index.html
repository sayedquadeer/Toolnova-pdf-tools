Our mission is to provide a clean, reliable, and free toolkit that anyone can use — from students and professionals to businesses of all sizes.

Answers to common questions about PDF Tools.

Is PDF Tools free to use?

Yes, PDF Tools is 100% free to use with no hidden charges or subscriptions required.

No installation is required. PDF Tools works directly in your web browser on any device.

PDF Tools is designed to process files in your browser, helping keep your documents private.

Can I use PDF Tools on mobile devices?

Yes, PDF Tools is fully responsive and works smoothly on phones, tablets, and desktops.

What file formats are supported?

Depending on the tool, PDF Tools supports PDF, Word, JPG, PNG and other common formats.

Since processing happens in your browser, limits mainly depend on your device's performance.
